# marketing

Everything for the Devpost submission and the 3-minute judging slot.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| [devpost.md](devpost.md) | The Devpost submission draft, field by field. Fill every `[FILL]` before Sun 11:00 |
| `assets/` | Screenshots, GIFs, the cover image, the architecture diagram and the demo video (see the checklist in devpost.md) |

Naming: `assets/YYYY-MM-DD-<what>.<ext>`, e.g. `assets/2026-11-08-board-reprice.png`.
Keep videos under ~50 MB or link them (YouTube or Drive) instead of committing them.
