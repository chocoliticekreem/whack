# Marshall Wace: the New Worlds track

Everything about our sponsor: the brief, how they judge, who they are, and what has won with
them before. Our idea is in [idea.md](idea.md).

## 1. The brief
> **Make a project that explores the idea of New Worlds.** Build something that opens a door to
> the unexplored, whether that's new data, ideas or something novel no one has seen before.
> Interpret the theme however you think best captures the spirit of this idea!
> Please come and chat to us if you're looking for inspiration!

> **We'd like to see:** Creativity · Technical complexity · Good presentation.
> **Do what you're interested in!**

| | |
|---|---|
| Prize | iPad + Apple Pencil, plus MW merch (T-shirt, fleece, gilet, North Face duffel). Per member or one per team is **unconfirmed** (the other sponsors all say "each member") |
| Judging | A strict **3-minute** slot with MW. Live code on a laptop, talked through |
| **Sat 14:00, OC0.01** | **A workshop, not a talk:** System Design, "Data Ingestion Platform", with its own prizes. Go as a team, then pitch our one-liner to them |

**Ask MW:** how the three criteria are weighted; whether the prize is per member; whether our
one-liner feels novel to them.

## 2. Who they are
- London alternatives manager, founded 1997 by Paul Marshall and Ian Wace. **$92.6bn AUM**,
  782+ people, **~200 developers**. Long/short equity, both quantitative and fundamental.
- "Technology and data have been at the core of our business for 25+ years." Flat and
  meritocratic: "as much responsibility and autonomy as you can handle".
- **TOPS (Trade Optimised Portfolio System), 2002:** MW's founding idea and the **first alpha
  capture platform**. Brokers submit trade ideas with a rationale, timeframe and conviction.
  MW measure each source's track record and weight ideas by who has been right. *We clone this
  in [idea.md](idea.md).*
- **They look for:** a strong communicator, passion for technology, critical thinking.
  **They don't need:** finance knowledge, or a CS degree.
- Roles: a 10-week internship (2027/28 grads), a 6-month AI placement, and a rotational grad
  scheme.

## 3. Their briefs over time
| Event | Theme | Their example directions | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| DurHack 2024 | Chain of events | a game where your actions have consequences · visualise global supply chains · a high-throughput data pipeline | Innovation/Idea, Creativity, Learning |
| DurHack 2025 | Bridging Worlds | map data between domains to uncover new insights · interface between independent systems · human-AI collaboration | Creativity/Innovation, Technical execution, Research/Learning, Polish |
| IC Hack 26 | Emergent Behaviour | not published | not published |
| **WHACK 26** | **New Worlds** | **none**: "something novel no one has seen before" | **Creativity, Technical complexity, Presentation** |

**What changed:** novelty is now in the brief itself, there are no example directions (they
don't want template answers), and "Learning" was dropped. The bar is original, hard, and
well sold.

## 5. Why they won (patterns)
1. **Emergent systems:** two of the last three awards went to multi-agent systems. They want
   to *see* many parts interact and something surprising come out.
2. **A playful hook on a real technical core:** memorable in 5 seconds, with genuine
   engineering underneath.
3. **Data mapped across domains, and predictions:** both keep coming up, and prediction is
   MW's business.
4. **Ordinary tech used in an unexpected way**, not new inventions: traffic lights as a data
   channel, a crystal ball as a model.
5. **Full teams, one strong demo, entered for several prizes.**
6. **What lost:** literal readings of the theme (the generic "bridge" apps in 2025).

**What this means for us:** Digital Twin (a simulated crowd) is our closest prior art. To beat
it we need a real city, live data, a visible outcome, and a mechanism MW recognise as their own.
