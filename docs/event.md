## How the event works (from the opening ceremony slides)
- **Pick ONE gold sponsor's problem statement per team.** Organisers collect team name + chosen
  sponsor during the first hour / after lunch. Building something that lines up with the
  statement is "in your best interest".
- **Submit on Devpost**, using the QR code on the slides. Project name = **group name**. Add every
  teammate, images and a description. Submit **after 9am Sunday**, before **11:00**. Submit even
  if it's unfinished: **no Devpost entry means no prizes**.
- **Judging: a strict 3-minute slot** with your chosen title sponsor, in a room and at a time
  they give you. "Present however you like". They recommend running code on a laptop and talking
  through it. No slides required.
- **Rules:** wear your lanyard. **After 22:00 the Oculus doors won't let you back in**; message on
  Discord to re-enter. Organisers wear white T-shirts. Help is on Discord (general help, or an
  anonymous ticket).
- **Organisers' tips:** "100% of the winners stayed all night". "Even if you spend 6 hours
  ideating that's OK". "Speak to the sponsors!". **"A smaller working product > a large half
  working product."**
- Scale: 400 hackers (doubled from 200), prize pool £6,000+ (up from £4,500).
